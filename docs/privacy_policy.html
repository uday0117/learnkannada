Privacy Policy for Learn Kannada

Application: Learn Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ ಕಲಿ) • Last Updated: October 21, 2026

Privacy Friendly
Key Summary: Learn Kannada is built with your privacy at the forefront. We do not require any user account or collect personal information. Your learning bookmarks, quiz scores, and settings are stored locally on your device. We use Google AdMob to display advertisements which help us keep this educational resource completely free.

1. Introduction

UK Solutions ("us", "we", or "our") operates the Learn Kannada mobile application (the "Service" or "App").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

For more information about UK Solutions, please visit our official website: https://ukrsolutions.in.

2. Information Collection and Storage

We do not directly collect, harvest, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII).

Learn Kannada functions primarily as an on-device language learning application:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email, phone number, or name to use the App.
  • Local Device Storage: Your learning progress, favorite words, bookmarked conversation sentences, quiz scores, theme preferences (light/dark mode), and speech rate settings are stored strictly in your device's local memory (via SharedPreferences).
  • No Cloud Syncing: None of your learning data is transmitted to or stored on our external servers.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To provide free access to educational content and support ongoing maintenance, Learn Kannada utilizes third-party SDKs:

Google AdMob (Google LLC)

We display banner and interstitial ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver contextual and personalized advertisements, Google AdMob may collect and process certain data including:

  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or IDFA)
  • IP address (used to infer general coarse location, e.g., city or region)
  • App performance data, diagnostic metrics, and ad interaction statistics
  • Operating system version and device hardware model

For comprehensive details on how Google handles advertising data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Partner Technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Managing Personalized Ads

You can manage or opt out of personalized advertisements at any time via your device settings:

  • Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Tap Delete advertising ID or enable Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Toggle off tracking permissions, or Apple Advertising > Turn off Personalized Ads.

4. App Permissions

Learn Kannada requests only the minimal system permissions strictly required for core functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Needed to retrieve advertisement banners from Google AdMob and maintain network connectivity checks.
  • AUDIO / TEXT-TO-SPEECH (TTS): Utilized locally via the Flutter TTS engine to pronounce Kannada words and sentences. Voice audio is synthesized on-device or via the system's native TTS provider.

We do NOT request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise GPS location, photos, or files.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our Service is designed as an educational, family-friendly tool suitable for learners of all ages, including students and children learning Kannada.

We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has inadvertently provided personal information to third-party services through our App, please contact us immediately and we will take prompt corrective action.

6. Data Security and Retention

Because all your personal app interactions, favorite vocabulary, and quiz achievements reside exclusively on your physical device:

  • Your data security is inherently protected by your personal device locks (PIN, password, biometrics).
  • If you clear the application data in Android/iOS settings or uninstall Learn Kannada, all locally saved progress and favorites will be permanently removed.
  • We do not retain backups of your individual learning records on remote servers.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Under international privacy regulations including GDPR and CCPA:

  • You have the right to access, export, or delete your data at any time by clearing your device storage.
  • You have the right to withdraw consent for personalized ad tracking through your operating system settings.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications in our features, operational practices, or legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" revision date.
